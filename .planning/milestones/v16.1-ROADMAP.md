# Milestone Roadmap: v16.1 Document-Anchored Tenancy Dates & Minimalist Register

## Overview

Anchor tenant and applicant start dates directly to their earliest document date in the archive (`MIN(primary_date)`), eliminating manual start date entry and timeline conflicts. For brand-new entities with zero documents, display "Auto (on first upload)" until their first document is ingested. Retain user control over the end date (Present vs. specific vacate/closing date). In the House Profile, streamline the tenant selection area into a clean, minimalist layout: all residents at the top, a single divider line, and all applicants at the bottom, using simple one-word labels.
