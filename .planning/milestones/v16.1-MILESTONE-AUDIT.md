## 1. Executive Summary

Milestone v16.1 achieved the auto-anchored tenancy model and minimalist register design requested by the user:
- Tenant and applicant start dates are automatically anchored to the earliest document in the digital archive (`MIN(primary_date)`), eliminating manual start date guessing and timeline date conflicts.
- For brand-new tenants/applicants with zero documents, start dates are nullable and display "Auto (on first upload)", snapping automatically to the document date upon first ingestion.
- The end date and Present checkbox remain administrative decisions controlled by the user in the House Settings modal.
- In the House Profile, the tenancy register has been streamlined to a clean, minimalist layout: resident tenants at the top (`المستأجرون`), a single subtle divider line, and applicants at the bottom (`المتقدمون`), with all wordy boilerplate removed.

All **8 requirements** across **DAT**, **REG**, and **VER** categories have been verified and validated with 100% passing tests (161 .NET xUnit and 293 Vitest tests).
