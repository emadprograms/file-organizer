# Milestone v15.0 Specification: Non-Residing Applicants & Prospective Tenancies Archive
**Architecture RFC & Design Contract**
**Date:** 2026-09-12
**Status:** Proposed / Under Discussion

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Context

In institutional, military, and municipal housing administration, house files frequently contain extensive documentation for individuals who **applied for housing or were issued allocation orders, but never actually took physical occupancy or resided in the property**.

A typical case involves:
- A 5-to-10-year span of administrative paperwork (e.g., 2014–2024):
  - Initial housing applications (`طلبات تخصيص السكن`)
  - Ministerial or administrative allocation decrees (`قرارات التخصيص`)
  - Civil status records, marriage certificates, children's birth certificates (`شهادات الميلاد والبيانات العائلية`)
  - Salary certificates, employment statements, and financial guarantors (`بيانات الراتب والكفالات`)
  - Follow-up appeals, status inquiries, and administrative correspondence (`المتابعات والمراسلات الرسمية`)
  - Eventual cancellation, withdrawal, or refusal to take possession (`إلغاء التخصيص / استنكاف / سحب`)

### The Problem in Current Architecture
1. **Tenure & Occupancy Distortion**: Currently, any tenant record entered with a start and end date is computed as a physical resident. A 10-year application trail erroneously flags the house as occupied for 10 years, coloring it red (`> 10 Yrs`), or prevents the house from properly displaying as `Vacant` (`رمادي / Grey`).
2. **Factually Misleading Tenancy Register**: In the House Tenancy Register (`سجل المستأجرين المتعاقبين`), the applicant is listed as an active or past occupant, which creates legal and administrative ambiguity during property audits.
3. **Ingest & Date Conflict Friction**: Ingesting documents across the applicant's 10-year timeline triggers false date-overlap warnings or conflicts against actual residents who may have occupied the house during those same calendar years.

Milestone v15.0 provides **first-class structural support for Non-Residing Applicants and Allocation Dossiers (`ملفات التخصيص والمتقدمين`)**, decoupling administrative paperwork from physical occupancy while preserving 100% document searchability, timeline ordering, and export capabilities.

## 2. Core Architectural Pillars

### Pillar 1: Relational Schema & Tenant Classification
We enhance the `tenants` entity with explicit status typing across both SQLite databases (`organizer.db`) and backends (FastAPI & ASP.NET Core 8.0).

```sql
-- Schema Migration: Add tenant_type and occupancy flags
ALTER TABLE tenants ADD COLUMN tenant_type TEXT DEFAULT 'resident';
-- Possible values:
--   'resident'             : Actual physical occupant (default)
--   'applicant_unfulfilled': Received allocation/applied, never lived in property (لم يستلم / لم يسكن)
--   'applicant_cancelled'  : Allocation revoked/cancelled (تخصيص ملغى)
--   'prospective'          : General applicant dossier under review (ملف تحت الدراسة)

ALTER TABLE tenants ADD COLUMN occupancy_status TEXT DEFAULT 'residing';
-- Possible values: 'residing', 'vacated', 'never_occupied'
```

- **DTO / Model Parity**:
  - Python: `TenantResponse.tenant_type: str`, `occupancy_status: str`
  - C# .NET: `HouseTenantDto.TenantType`, `HouseTenantDto.OccupancyStatus`

---

### Pillar 2: Strict Exclusion from Tenure & Vacancy Engine
Non-residing applicants are strictly segregated from the property occupancy engine:
- **House Vacancy**: If a house contains 0 active `resident` tenants, the house is guaranteed to render as **`Vacant` (`رمادي / Grey`)**, even if it has an active applicant dossier.
- **Tenure Duration Calculation**: Tenure badges (< 5 yrs: green, 5–10 yrs: yellow, > 10 yrs: red) are **only** computed from `resident` records. Applicants never influence house card borders or tenure statistics.
- **Overlap Tolerance**: Because an applicant never physically lived in the house, an applicant dossier spanning 2014–2024 **can coexist chronologically** with a real tenant who actually occupied the property during 2016–2018 without throwing occupancy conflict errors.

---

### Pillar 3: Segregated Tenancy Register UX (`سجل المستأجرين المتعاقبين`)
The House Profile view (`house-profile.js`) cleanly distinguishes actual occupants from applicant dossiers:
1. **Two Distinct Sections**:
   - **سجل الإشغال الفعلي (Actual Occupants)**: Primary chronological list showing physical residents, tenure duration, and move-in/vacate dates.
   - **ملفات التخصيص والمتقدمين (Applicants & Allocation Dossiers)**: Dedicated sub-section or tab for individuals who had paperwork but never moved in.
2. **Distinct Visual Badging**:
   - Applicant cards feature a distinct indigo/purple badge: `📁 ملف تخصيص (لم يسكن) • Allocation Dossier`.
   - Lifespan is labeled appropriately: `فترة المتابعة: 2014 - 2024 (10 سنوات)` instead of "فترة السكن / Tenure".
3. **Tenant Management Modal**:
   - In House Settings (`house-settings-modal.js`), the manager can toggle a row between `مستأجر فعلي (Resident)` and `طلب تخصيص (Applicant - Never Occupied)`.

---

### Pillar 4: Ingest Station & Batch Routing
- **Tenant Dropdown Badging**:
  - In Ingest Station (`ingest-station.js`), options are formatted with clear semantic indicators:
    - `🟢 أحمد المنصوري (2021 - Present) [Resident]`
    - `👤 خالد العتيبي (2018 - 2020) [Vacated]`
    - `📋 سالم الشامسي (2014 - 2024) [ملف تخصيص / Applicant]`
- **Smart Date Range Validation**:
  - For applicants, the system validates against the `application_span` (e.g. 2014–2024). Documents within that 10-year span are accepted smoothly.
  - If a document is dated after the application end date (e.g. 2026), the system asks: `"Applicant '{name}' application file was closed on {end_date}. Do you want to extend his application records to {doc_date}?"`.

---

### Pillar 5: Archive Export & PDF Dossier Segregation
- **Export Options Modal (`#export-archive-modal`)**:
  - Scope dropdown includes:
    - `🏛️ كامل سجل المنزل (Full Archive - All Residents & Applicants)`
    - `🏡 المستأجرون الفعليون فقط (Actual Occupants Only)`
    - Individual applicant dossiers (e.g. `📋 سالم الشامسي (ملف تخصيص 2014 - 2024)`)
- **PDF Dossier Running Footer**:
  - Pages for non-residing applicants clearly note dossier type in the running footer:
    `Bottom-Center: 05 - عقود | ملف تخصيص (لم يسكن)`

## 3. Phased Implementation Roadmap

| Phase | Title | Scope & Deliverables |
|---|---|---|
| **Phase 109** | **Database Layer & Entity Classification** | Add `tenant_type` and `occupancy_status` to SQLite schema, automatic migrations in FastAPI and ASP.NET Core, update repository queries, and unit tests. |
| **Phase 110** | **Tenure Engine & Vacancy Isolation** | Decouple non-residing applicants from `DurationCategory`, house card borders, and vacancy status; ensure houses with only applicants render `Vacant (Grey)`. |
| **Phase 111** | **House Tenancy Register & Settings UI** | Add "Applicants & Allocation Dossiers" section to House Profile, distinctive indigo badging, and tenant type selector in House Settings modal. |
| **Phase 112** | **Ingest Station Integration & Date Validation** | Support applicant dossier routing in Ingest Station, 10-year date span validation, and batch broadcast support. |
| **Phase 113** | **Export Pipeline & Milestone Verification** | Dossier export filtering (Residents vs Applicants), running footer distinction, Playwright E2E and multi-stack audit. |
