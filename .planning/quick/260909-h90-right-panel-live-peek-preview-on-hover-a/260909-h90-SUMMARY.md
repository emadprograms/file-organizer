# Quick Task Summary: Right Panel Live Peek on Hover & macOS Spacebar Quick Look

## Overview
Engineered a seamless, dual-mode document inspection experience inspired by macOS Finder:
1. **Ambient Zero-Click Live Peek**: Moving your cursor over any document row in Categories View or Timeline View updates the right-hand viewer panel in real time (250ms debounce) with zero floating overlays over the list.
2. **Focused macOS Quick Look Modal**: Preserved the centered, high-resolution dark-glass modal triggered via Spacebar or the Eye (`👁️`) button for deep inspection, small print reading, and zoom.

## Key Changes Implemented

1. **Right Panel Live Peek (`doc-viewer.js`, `pdf-preview.js`, `index.html`)**
   - Added `peekDocument(vaultId, title, doc)` to smoothly display the document PDF in `#document-viewer-panel` on hover without locking or disturbing selection.
   - Added `#viewer-peek-badge` in the right panel header to distinguish live peeking from permanently pinned documents.
   - Retired and disabled `#pdf-preview-tooltip` (`display: none !important`), completely eliminating intrusive floating popups over the document list.

2. **100% Free 3-Dot Menus (`categories-view.js`, `timeline-view.js`)**
   - Hovering over `.doc-menu-btn` (the 3-dot action button) immediately calls `cancelPeek()`.
   - The action menus (Rename, Move, Copy) are totally unobstructed, comfortable to click, and free of any hover conflicts.

3. **macOS-Style Quick Look Modal**
   - Retained `#quick-look-modal` with centered layout, dark-glass backdrop, metadata badges, "Open Viewer" transfer button, and keyboard hints.
   - Triggerable via `Spacebar` on any hovered or selected document, or by clicking the `👁️` button on the card.
   - Pressing `Spacebar` or `Escape` closes the modal immediately.
