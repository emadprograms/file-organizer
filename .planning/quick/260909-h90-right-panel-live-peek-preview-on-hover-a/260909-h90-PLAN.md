# Quick Task: Right Panel Live Peek on Hover & macOS Spacebar Quick Look

## Description
Deliver a zero-click document inspection experience by rendering live document previews directly inside the right-hand viewer panel on hover, while preserving the premium centered macOS Quick Look modal triggered by Spacebar and the eye button:
1. **Right-Panel Live Peek (Zero-Click)**:
   - Hovering over a document card smoothly updates the right-hand viewer panel (`#document-viewer-panel`) without requiring any clicks.
   - Completely removes floating tooltip overlays over the document list so nothing ever blocks the 3-dot menu or obstructs rows.
   - Clicking a document pins/locks it in the viewer.
2. **Focused macOS Quick Look Modal (Deep Inspection)**:
   - Retains the centered dark-glass modal for immersive inspection with full metadata badges.
   - Triggerable via Spacebar on any hovered/selected document or by clicking the subtle Eye button (`👁️`).
   - Pressing Space or Esc immediately closes it.
3. **Menu Accessibility**:
   - 3-dot menu buttons remain 100% free and unobstructed at all times.
